# SoloQ — Mythic+ applicant triage for your own key

SoloQ helps Mythic+ key holders run their own Premade Group Finder listing — it
scores incoming applicants against your minimum M+ rating, builds a complete group
(tank/healer/DPS, with optional Lust and Battle Res), and turns inviting into one click.

It is a Retail-only companion for leaders listing **their own Mythic+ key**. SoloQ adds
a side panel to Blizzard's applicant viewer that reads each solo applicant's role and
overall Mythic+ rating, tells you who to invite, and assembles a full group around you —
all from your existing listing.

## Features

- **Minimum-score gate** — set one minimum Mythic+ rating; applicants at or above it
  are flagged to invite, the rest to decline.
- **One-click actions** — SoloQ recommends, you click. Invite/Decline act on the
  highlighted applicant; actions stay click-driven to respect Blizzard's protections.
- **Proposed group builder** — fills a full 5-player comp (one tank, one healer, three
  DPS) around your own role and anyone already in your party. *Invite Group* lights up
  once a complete, passing group is ready.
- **Composition requirements** — optionally require **Bloodlust/Heroism** and a
  **Battle Res** in the proposed group.
- **Group applicants** — queued duos/trios are included when every member passes.
- **Prep Key button** — a "SoloQ: Prep Key" button under Premade Groups preps your
  owned keystone listing; then click Blizzard's *List Group* to post it.

## Usage

- List your key (or hit **SoloQ: Prep Key**), open the applicant viewer, and the SoloQ
  panel appears for the group leader.
- Enable evaluation, set your minimum score, and toggle Lust / Battle Res as desired.
- `/soloq` toggles the panel · `/soloq reset` restores defaults · `/soloq debug` toggles logging.

## Compatibility

- Retail only — supports game versions **12.0.7** and 12.0.5.
